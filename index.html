About Me

I'm a software developer with over half a decade of experience, driven by curiosity and a passion for building innovative solutions. I've been fortunate to work on diverse projects ranging from blockchain protocols to CLI tools, always seeking to learn and grow.

My expertise in Rust has become a cornerstone of my development journey, and I believe it will be valuable to forward-thinking companies like Qdrant. I'm deeply committed to the open source philosophy and love giving back to the community through dev tools and contributions.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me rock climbing and bouldering with some of the best climbers in the country, exploring new locations on my motorcycle, or backpacking across countries. I've backpacked through five countries in the last year and hope to see many more. Recently, I've discovered the joy of running and have been steadily increasing my mileage every week.

The amalgamation of open source, dev tools, and Rust makes opportunities like this closely aligned with what I want to pursue in my career. I'm excited about the possibility of contributing to innovative projects that push the boundaries of what's possible.
